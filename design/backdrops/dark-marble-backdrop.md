# Dark Luxury Marble Backdrop — Saved Design Brief

Reusable style brief for future image generation. Reference image: `dark-marble-backdrop.png`.

## Generation prompt

Empty studio backdrop, ultra-realistic dark luxury product photography background. A near-black marble back wall and a near-black marble tabletop surface, meeting at a horizontal edge about one-third down from the top of the frame. Deep jet-black stone (approx. #0F0F10 to #1B1B1D) with sparse, elegant, thin veins in glowing copper and rose-gold (approx. #B5693A to #D9946A), long and angular, branching and fading in and out, with a few brighter veins catching the light on the tabletop. Polished satin surface with a subtle soft reflection and gentle highlights along the veins. Dramatic low-key lighting: a soft spotlight pool on the center of the table fading into a darker vignette at the edges and corners, a faint atmospheric haze behind the center of the scene, clean empty center for product placement. Camera at a slightly elevated front angle (about 20 degrees), shallow depth of field, crisp stone texture, high contrast, moody, premium coffee-shop and dessert-photography aesthetic. 3:2 landscape, 8K, sharp focus, photorealistic.
